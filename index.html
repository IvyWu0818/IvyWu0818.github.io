Congestion-Aware SFC Scheduling for Industrial IoT under Bursty Traffic

Jia-Rong Lin, Chin-Yu Wu, Ping-Chih Liu, Shi-Sheng Sun
WASN 2026Best Paper Awardin Chinese

The first version of the bursty-traffic scheduling study, presented at the joint MC 2026 and WASN 2026 conference, National Chung Hsing University, August 2026. The paper is written in Chinese with an English abstract.

English abstract

Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) applications often generate bursty uplink traffic under event-driven scenarios, causing instantaneous congestion in Service Function Chain (SFC) queues at edge nodes and degrading service latency and Quality of Service (QoS). This paper proposes a Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) based dynamic scheduling mechanism for IIoT edge nodes with pre-deployed Virtual Network Functions (VNFs). We construct a two-tier queuing model comprising a global buffer and per-VNF stage queues and characterize bursty arrivals using a two-state compound Poisson process. The scheduling problem is formulated as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) and solved by the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm, which outputs the release amount of each VNF. Simulation results show that the proposed method effectively reduces queuing delay and QoS violation rate under bursty traffic.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for End-to-End Latency Optimization in NOMA–MEC-Enabled IIoT

Chin-Yu Wu, Jia-Rong Lin, Ping-Chih Liu, Shi-Sheng Sun
MC 2026posterfirst authorin Chinese

The first version of the IoT Journal work, using a TD3 agent, which I presented as a poster in August 2026. The paper is written in Chinese with an English abstract.

English abstract

In Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) environments, multi-stage real-time services such as preprocessing, anomaly detection, model inference, and feedback control form workflow-style processing chains. Insufficient resources at any stage can cause queuing effects to accumulate along the chain, increasing end-to-end (E2E) latency and the risk of task timeouts, jointly affected by access-layer and edge-computing congestion. This paper models the multi-stage processing flow as a Service Function Chain (SFC) under a Non-Orthogonal Multiple Access (NOMA) wireless transmission environment, where the power allocation and Successive Interference Cancellation (SIC) decoding order follow fixed rules and subchannel assignment serves as the only NOMA-related decision variable, and focuses on system-level joint decisions: subchannel assignment, task offloading, edge-computing resource scheduling, and Virtual Network Function (VNF) placement. Since the joint optimization constitutes a Mixed-Integer Nonlinear Programming (MINLP) problem and is NP-hard, we employ the Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic policy gradient (TD3) deep reinforcement learning to design an online decision-making mechanism, with greedy and genetic algorithms as baselines. Simulation results validate that the proposed method outperforms the baselines in both E2E latency and resource efficiency.

AI Chess Live Commentary Agent

Grand Prizeteam of threeLangGraph · Stockfish · python-chess · GPT-4o · TTS · ChromaDB · FastAPI

A real-time chess broadcaster built as a LangGraph pipeline. python-chess tracks the board and Stockfish scores every move; a Director agent turns the change in evaluation into a severity level (routine, notable, or critical) that sets how deep the analysis goes and the commentators' tone.

  • The game never waits. Each move returns to play in about 0.07 s; a separate worker thread drains a job queue and, based on backlog and severity, cuts in on critical moments, queues notable ones, and stays silent on routine moves.
  • Two hosts, one script. One LLM call writes an alternating two-host dialogue, which is then voiced sentence by sentence with text-to-speech.
  • Grounded commentary. The hosts may only repeat facts from an engine-built facts packet, so they cannot invent evaluations; layered memory keeps the story of the game, and ablation tests compared variants.
